# How we work together

Every engagement starts the same way and moves at the pace that makes
sense for your business. There's no fixed package to buy off a menu here;
the plan gets built around what's actually slowing you down.

## 1. Discovery Call

A free conversation, no pitch. We talk through where the manual work is
piling up, what's costing you the most time, and whether this is a good
fit. Most owners walk away from this call with a clearer picture of the
problem even before any work starts.

## 2. Custom Plan

An audit of the systems you're running now: your CRM, your bookkeeping,
your scheduling, and anything in between held together by spreadsheets or
memory. From that, a specific plan for what gets connected, what gets
automated, and what reporting gets built, so you can see the business
clearly instead of guessing.

## 3. Ongoing Partnership

Implementation and support after the plan is set. Systems need upkeep,
not just a one-time setup, so this is where the work gets built, tested,
and kept running.

## The kind of work this includes

- Connecting a CRM like Jobber to your bookkeeping in QuickBooks, so a job
  doesn't have to be entered three times by three different people.
- Building trade-specific reports, like lead-source performance or job
  profitability, that generic field service software doesn't provide.
- Cutting out the manual re-entry and double-checking that eats hours
  every week without ever showing up as a line item.

## What you'll walk away with

- A fully documented operations playbook your crew can actually follow
- Delegation structures so you're not the one every question routes through
- Metrics and reports your team owns, not just numbers you check alone
- Confidence that the business runs whether or not you're in the truck that day
- Time back in your week for growth, bidding, or life outside the business

## Is this right for you?

This works best when:

- You've got a crew (even a small one) and need to stop doing everything yourself
- You're generating steady revenue but feel like you're running on a hamster wheel
- You've tried delegating before, but it never quite stuck
- You know you need better systems, but don't know where to start

## Investment

Monthly retainer, starting at **$1,000–$1,500/month**. Scope depends on the size of your operation and how deep the engagement goes.

The first step is always a free discovery call to understand your situation and figure out if it's a good fit.
