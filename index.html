What "connecting your systems" actually means

This isn't a chatbot and it isn't a generic dashboard. It's practical work: linking your CRM (Jobber and similar tools) to your bookkeeping (QuickBooks) so nobody is re-typing the same job into three places. It's building reports your specific trade needs, like which lead sources actually convert or which jobs are profitable, that off-the-shelf field service software is too broad to give you out of the box.

What changes once the systems are running

  • Clear, documented processes your crew can follow without you on the phone
  • Reports that show what's actually happening in the business, not guesses
  • Time back in your week for growth, family, and the reasons you started this business
  • Confidence that a week off doesn't mean a week of catching up when you're back
