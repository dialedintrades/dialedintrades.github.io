# I ran into this problem before I started fixing it for other people

I owned and ran an irrigation business. I know what it's like to have every
job, every callback, and every invoice question land on your desk because
there's no system built to catch it first. I also know what it costs: the
long hours, the missed dinners, the feeling that the business can't survive
a week without you in it.

Before that, I spent more than twenty years in software, with a computer
science degree and an MBA behind me. That combination is the reason Dialed
In Trades exists. Most operations consultants can tell you what's wrong.
Most software developers can build you something, but don't understand the
trade well enough to know what's worth building. I can do both, because
I've stood in both places.

## What I actually do

I connect the tools you already use, like your CRM and your bookkeeping
software, so information moves between them without a person doing it by
hand. I build reporting specific to your trade, not a generic dashboard,
so you can see what's actually working: which lead sources convert, which
jobs make money, where the bottlenecks really are.

## Why it matters

A skilled tradesperson shouldn't be trapped running admin work they're bad
at. You built this business to do the work you're good at. My job is to
get the rest of it out of your way.

## What I believe

**Systems beat heroics.** The owner working 80-hour weeks isn't a hero,
they're a single point of failure. Build systems so the business can run
without you.

**Your business should work without you. Not someday, now.** If you left
for a month, would the wheels come off? If so, that's not a time problem,
it's a systems problem.

## Background

- MBA, BYU
- BS in Computer Science, BYU
- Certified Scrum Master, Scrum Alliance
- 20+ years in software development before running my own trades business
