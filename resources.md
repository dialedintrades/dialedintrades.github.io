# Tools and notes for running your operation

Practical resources for trades owners working on systems, reporting, and getting out of the bottleneck seat.

## Tools I recommend

A few starting points for connecting your systems:

- **CRM / field service** — Jobber or a similar tool to run scheduling, dispatch, and job tracking in one place.
- **Bookkeeping** — QuickBooks, kept in sync with your CRM so a job never gets typed in three times.
- **Reporting** — trade-specific reports (lead-source performance, job profitability) that generic field service dashboards don't give you out of the box.
